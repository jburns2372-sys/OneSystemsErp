## 1. Role Description

### Who Is This Guide For?

This guide is designed for **any person joining the organization** who has been granted access to OneSystemsERP for the first time. Regardless of your final assigned role — whether you are a Project Manager, Purchasing Officer, Accountant, or Site Engineer — this guide walks you through the foundational knowledge you need before touching any module.

### What Is OneSystemsERP?

OneSystemsERP is an **AI-enhanced Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system** purpose-built for large-scale construction and engineering projects. It integrates the following operational domains into a single platform:

| Domain | Modules |
|--------|---------|
| **Project Execution** | Projects, BOQ Management, Accomplishments, Variation Orders |
| **Procurement & Logistics** | Material Requests, Purchase Orders, Deliveries, Inventory, Material Issuance |
| **Finance & Accounting** | Finance, Supplier Payables, Progress Billings, Collections, Petty Cash, Expenses |
| **Human Resources** | Workers Database, Payroll, Payroll Payments |
| **Subcontracting** | Subcontractors, Work Packages, Invoicing, Progress Tracking |
| **Equipment Management** | Fleet Registry, Deployments, Utilization, Maintenance, Fuel Tracking |
| **AI & Intelligence** | AI Command Center, AI Validation Engine, AI ERP Assistant, Knowledge Center |
| **Administration** | Users, Roles & Permissions, System Settings, System Audit, Documents |

### Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> OneSystemsERP enforces **strict Role-Based Access Control**. Your assigned role determines:
> - Which sidebar modules are visible to you
> - Which buttons and actions are enabled
> - Which records you can create, edit, approve, or delete
> - Which reports and dashboards you can access

You cannot bypass these restrictions. If you believe your access is incorrect, contact your System Administrator.

## 4. Step-by-Step Common Tasks

### 4.1 First Login

1. Open your web browser (Chrome or Edge recommended).
2. Navigate to the OneSystemsERP URL provided by your administrator.
3. Enter your **email address** and **temporary password**.
4. Click **Sign In**.
5. You will be redirected to your role-specific **Dashboard**.

### 4.2 Changing Your Password

1. Click on your **profile avatar** in the top-right corner of the sidebar.
2. Select **Profile**.
3. Navigate to the **Security** section.
4. Enter your current password and your new password (minimum 8 characters, with at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character).
5. Click **Save Changes**.

### 4.3 Understanding Your Dashboard

Your dashboard is **dynamically configured** based on your role archetype:

| Archetype | Dashboard Shows |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **EXECUTIVE** | AI overrides pending, total projects, contract budget, outstanding liabilities |
| **PM** | Active BOQ consolidations, accomplishments tracked, site requisitions (MRF) |
| **FINANCE** | Unpaid payables, petty cash balances, active payroll periods |
| **PROCUREMENT** | Pending MRFs, open canvassing, active POs, pending deliveries |
| **LOGISTICS** | Expected deliveries, material issuances, supplier count |
| **HR** | Active payroll periods, worker database count, system users |
| **AUDIT** | System audit logs, AI validation count |
| **FIELD** | Daily time records, active job orders |

### 4.4 Navigating the Sidebar

1. The **left sidebar** contains all modules visible to your role.
2. Click the **« icon** to collapse/expand the sidebar for more screen space.
3. Modules are grouped by functional area (e.g., Procurement, Finance, HR).
4. Modules you do not have access to will **not appear** in your sidebar — this is by design.

### 4.5 Using the AI ERP Assistant

1. Look for the **💬 AI Assistant** button in the bottom-right corner or sidebar.
2. Click to open the chat interface.
3. Type your question in **plain English** (e.g., *"What is the remaining balance of wire?"* or *"Show me all pending MRFs"*).
4. The AI will search the system and respond with relevant data.

> [!TIP]
> The AI Assistant is available to **all roles**. It's the fastest way to find information without navigating through multiple modules.

### 4.6 Uploading a Document

1. Navigate to the **Documents** module from the sidebar.
2. Click **Upload File**.
3. Select the file from your computer (PDF, Excel, Image, or Word).
4. Add a **description** and assign the appropriate **category**.
5. Click **Submit**. The file is now stored in the centralized vault.

### 4.7 Viewing Reports

1. Navigate to the **Reports** module from the sidebar.
2. Select the report category (Financial, Procurement, Project, etc.).
3. Configure any filters (date range, project, status).
4. Click **Generate Report**.
5. Reports can be downloaded as **PDF** or **Excel**.

## 6. Common Mistakes to Avoid

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct Approach |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Sharing your login credentials with a colleague | Each user must have their own unique login. Sharing violates the audit trail. |
| Forgetting to log out on shared computers | Always log out when you step away. The system records all actions under your account. |
| Ignoring the "Access Denied" message | This is RBAC enforcing your role. Contact your admin if you believe it's an error. |
| Skipping the dashboard and going directly to modules | Your dashboard surfaces critical alerts and pending actions. Check it first every day. |
| Typing vague questions into the AI Assistant | Be specific. Instead of *"Show me stuff"*, ask *"Show me all POs pending delivery for Project Alpha"*. |
| Uploading files without descriptions | Undescribed files are nearly impossible to find later. Always add meaningful descriptions. |
| Not reading system notifications | Notifications contain pending approvals, delivery alerts, and workflow updates. Check them daily. |

## 8. Best Practices

### Security
- ✅ Change your password within the first hour of receiving your account.
- ✅ Use a unique, strong password (not reused from other systems).
- ✅ Never share your credentials — even with your supervisor.
- ✅ Log out when leaving your workstation.

### Productivity
- ✅ Bookmark the ERP URL in your browser for quick access.
- ✅ Check your Dashboard first thing every morning.
- ✅ Use the AI Assistant for quick lookups instead of scrolling through modules.
- ✅ Set browser notifications to receive real-time alerts.

### Data Quality
- ✅ Double-check all data before submitting forms.
- ✅ Attach supporting documents to all transactions (receipts, photos, blueprints).
- ✅ Use the correct project when logging transactions — cross-project entries can corrupt financial reports.

### Communication
- ✅ If something seems wrong, report it immediately — don't try to fix it yourself if you're unsure.
- ✅ Ask your supervisor before performing any irreversible action (deletions, approvals, locks).
- ✅ Document any system issues with screenshots and submit to IT/Admin.
