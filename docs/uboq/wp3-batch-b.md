  3. Hardened `previewSavedLayout()` in `src/lib/uboq/templates/saved-layouts.ts` to pass `resolveNarrowestFieldRole(caps.effectiveRole, caps.fieldAccessRole)` into `projectTemplateRows`, filter `systemMetaFields` via `filterSystemMetaFieldsForRoles`, and enforce `canRoleReadField` on `approvedLineTotal`.
  4. Verified in `WP3-BB-IR-01` (`scripts/uboq/verify-wp3-batch-b-corrections.ts`) across `SITE_ENGINEER`, narrowed `SITE_ENGINEER`, narrowed `VIEWER`, `EXTERNAL_VIEWER`, `BILLING_ENGINEER`, and `FINANCE_DIRECTOR`.

### `IR-02` — Historical Billing Authority & Carried-Forward Reconciliation (`HIGH`)
- **Finding**: Selecting the latest approved snapshot (`ORDER BY versionNumber DESC LIMIT 1`) could retroactively reprice historical `T5` accomplishments or `T6` progress billings after a newer BOQ snapshot or contract revision was approved, allow unverified/post-dated contract associations, or permit understated `previousQuantityBilled` to mask cumulative overbilling.
- **Closure**:
  1. Refactored `loadApprovedSnapshotPhaseBridge` in `src/lib/uboq/templates/canonical-source-adapters.ts` to resolve the single historical `UboqOperationalSnapshot` (`status IN ('APPROVED', 'SUPERSEDED')`) with `approvedAt <= periodCutoffDate` (`accomplishmentDate` for `T5`, `billingPeriodTo` for `T6`) that was not yet superseded as of `periodCutoffDate` via `supersedesSnapshotId` lineage:
     - Fails closed (`409 TEMPLATE_HISTORICAL_SNAPSHOT_NOT_FOUND` / `FORMAL_HISTORICAL_SNAPSHOT_NOT_FOUND`) when no approved snapshot existed on or before the certificate cutoff.
     - Fails closed (`409 TEMPLATE_HISTORICAL_SNAPSHOT_AMBIGUOUS` / `FORMAL_HISTORICAL_SNAPSHOT_AMBIGUOUS`) when multiple parallel snapshots exist at the cutoff without explicit `supersedesSnapshotId` lineage.
     - Requires a verified `UboqContractAuthority` (`status = 'APPROVED'`, `approvedById IS NOT NULL`, `approvedAt <= periodCutoffDate`) linked on `snapshot.contractAuthorityId` and reconciles `T6` `Billing.contractAmount` / `revisedContractAmount` against the resolved snapshot/contract baseline (`409 TEMPLATE_CONTRACT_RATE_ASSOCIATION_UNVERIFIED` / `FORMAL_CONTRACT_RATE_ASSOCIATION_UNVERIFIED`).
     - Excludes post-cutoff `VariationOrderApproval` deltas and prevents current `AwardedBOQItem.approvedClientVoQuantity` fallback on superseded historical snapshots.
  2. Added chronological prior-certificate chain reconciliation in `loadT5AccomplishmentCertCanonicalData` and `loadT6ProgressBillingCanonicalData`:
     - Computes authoritative cumulative prior certified/billed quantities across earlier valid certificates (`>= earliestSnapshotApprovedAt`).
     - Enforces `reconciledPrev = max(statedPrev, priorAuthCum)` against `effectiveApprovedQty` first (`409 TEMPLATE_OVERBILLING_CEILING_EXCEEDED` / `FORMAL_OVERBILLING_CEILING_EXCEEDED`), blocking understated `previousQuantityBilled` overbilling.
     - Rejects carried-forward discrepancies (`statedPrev != priorAuthCum`) with `409 TEMPLATE_PREVIOUS_BILLING_RECONCILIATION_MISMATCH` / `FORMAL_PREVIOUS_BILLING_RECONCILIATION_MISMATCH`.
  3. Enforced historical `@v<N>` snapshotcutoff, non-supersession, non-ambiguity, and verified contract-authority guards at the PostgreSQL trigger layer in **Forward-Only Migration `#29`**.
  4. Verified in `WP3-BB-IR-02` across two approved snapshots (`@v1` at `6,000.00` vs `@v2` at `6,500.00`), two sequential certificates, understated previous billed overbilling, carried-forward mismatch, pre-snapshot/unlinked/ambiguous/header-mismatch `409` cases, and direct SQL `P0001` trigger guards.

### `IR-03` — Inspection Quantity Integrity & Multi-Inspection Deduplication (`MEDIUM`)
- **Finding**: Checking only `EXISTS (Inspection WHERE approvalStatus = 'APPROVED')` did not prove approved inspected quantities (`Inspection.approvedQuantity`) were sufficient to cover positive certified claims, cap over-inspected items at the approved claim, or prevent duplicate counting across multiple inspections on the same `AccomplishmentItem`.
- **Closure**:
  1. Added `verifyApprovedInspectionsForClaim` in `src/lib/uboq/templates/canonical-source-adapters.ts`:
     - Rejects missing approved inspections (`409 TEMPLATE_MISSING_INSPECTION_EVIDENCE` / `FORMAL_MISSING_INSPECTION_EVIDENCE`).
     - Rejects multiple approved inspections on a single `AccomplishmentItem` unless every approved inspection carries a distinct, non-blank lot/scope linkage key (`remarks` or `inspectionFindings`, case-insensitively deduplicated) (`409 TEMPLATE_DUPLICATE_INSPECTION_LINKAGE` / `FORMAL_DUPLICATE_INSPECTION_LINKAGE`).
     - Sums deduplicated `Inspection.approvedQuantity` across distinct lots and rejects `totalApprovedInspectedDec < rawClaimDec` (`409 TEMPLATE_INSPECTION_QUANTITY_INSUFFICIENT` / `FORMAL_INSPECTION_QUANTITY_INSUFFICIENT`).
     - Caps `certifiedDec = min(rawClaimDec, totalApprovedInspectedDec)` so an inspection exceeding the claim never inflates certified SWA output above the approved claim.
  2. Enforced identical multi-lot distinctness and `SUM(approvedQuantity) >= claim` checks inside `public.uboq_guard_template_audit_event()` in **Forward-Only Migration `#29`**.
  3. Verified in `WP3-BB-IR-03` across `<` claim (`409` + SQL `P0001`), `==` claim (`200`), `>` claim (`200` capped at claim), valid multi-lot sum (`200`), duplicate multi-lot key (`409` + SQL `P0001`), and blank multi-lot linkage (`409` + SQL `P0001`).

### `IR-04` — `T7_SCHEDULE_EVM` Unified As-Of Cutoff & Progress Basis Separation (`MEDIUM`)
- **Finding**: `T7_SCHEDULE_EVM` needed one unified reporting cutoff (`asOfCutoffDate`) across `PV`, `EV`, and `AC`, strict exclusion of future-dated and indirect/unrelated records, and explicit separation of certified accomplishment progress from unverified self-reported `%`.
- **Closure**:
  1. Updated `loadT7ScheduleEvmCanonicalData` in `src/lib/uboq/templates/canonical-source-adapters.ts` to compute one unified `resolvedCutoffDate` (`<= now()`), anchored to verified certified accomplishments / certified progress updates when present so unverified self-reported `%` updates or future-dated records never shift the `PV`/`AC` cutoff.
  2. Enforced `resolvedCutoffDate` simultaneously across:
     - **`PV`**: activities starting after `resolvedCutoffDate` receive `0.0000%` planned progress and `0.00` `planned_value`.
     - **`EV`**: only `APPROVED` `Accomplishment` records (`accomplishmentDate <= resolvedCutoffDate`) passing `verifyApprovedInspectionsForClaim` and certified `ScheduleProgressUpdate` rows (`updateDate <= resolvedCutoffDate`) contribute to `actual_progress_pct` and `earned_value`. Unverified self-reported `%` updates receive `actual_progress_pct = 0.0000` and `earned_value = 0.00`, with explicit lineage in `as_of_cutoff_date`, `progress_verification_basis` (`UNVERIFIED_SELF_REPORTED_EXCLUDED_FROM_EV`), and `self_reported_progress_pct`.
     - **`AC`**: excludes `ProjectCostLedger` rows with `costDate > resolvedCutoffDate`, `INDIRECT`/`OVERHEAD`/`GENERAL_ADMIN`/`UNALLOCATED` categories, non-`APPROVED` status, and off-schedule BOQ items.
  3. Verified in `WP3-BB-IR-04`.

### `WP3-BB-IR2-01` — Non-Skipping Prior Approved Certificate Reconciliation (`HIGH`)
- **Finding (`OSERP-WP3-BATCH-B-IR2-2026-11-14`)**: In `src/lib/uboq/templates/canonical-source-adapters.ts`, the `T5` (`loadT5AccomplishmentCertCanonicalData`) and `T6` (`loadT6ProgressBillingCanonicalData`) historical reconciliation loops marked earlier `APPROVED` records with `isValidPrior = false` and skipped them when inspection sufficiency, carried-forward `previousQuantity` alignment, pre-snapshot cutoff, missing approver, or cumulative ceiling checks failed. Skipping an invalid earlier approved record allowed it to disappear from the cumulative historical baseline so a subsequent certificate could understate historical totals or pass cumulative ceiling checks.
- **Closure**:
  1. Removed all skip/drop paths (`isValidPrior = false`) from the prior approved certificate loops in both `loadT5AccomplishmentCertCanonicalData` and `loadT6ProgressBillingCanonicalData` (`src/lib/uboq/templates/canonical-source-adapters.ts`):
     - Queries all earlier `APPROVED` accomplishments (ordered by `accomplishmentDate ASC, createdAt ASC, id ASC`) and all earlier `APPROVED` / `APPROVED_FOR_PAYMENT` / `PAID` billings (including any row with missing `approvedById` so it cannot evade reconciliation, ordered by `billingPeriodTo ASC, billingDate ASC, createdAt ASC, id ASC`) on `projectId`.
     - Accumulates `historicalCertifiedByBoqId` (`T5`) and `authoritativePriorBilledByBoqId` (`T6`) monotonically across every earlier approved record (`nonDecreasingBase = max(existingCum, max(0, statedPrev))`, `nextCum = nonDecreasingBase + effectiveClaimedOrCertifiedQty`) so an invalid earlier approved record never disappears from the cumulative baseline.
     - Records audit-safe, non-financial diagnostic entries (`priorUnresolvedIssues` / `priorBillingUnresolvedIssues` containing only certificate IDs, item/BOQ IDs, ISO dates, reason codes, and 4dp quantities — zero unit rates, tax profiles, or currency amounts).
  2. Enforces cumulative overbilling ceiling checks (`409 TEMPLATE_OVERBILLING_CEILING_EXCEEDED` / `FORMAL_OVERBILLING_CEILING_EXCEEDED`) against the non-reduced historical baseline first, then rejects any certificate when `priorUnresolvedIssues.length > 0` (`409 TEMPLATE_PRIOR_CERTIFICATE_UNRESOLVED` / `FORMAL_PRIOR_CERTIFICATE_UNRESOLVED`), followed by carried-forward continuity enforcement (`409 TEMPLATE_PREVIOUS_BILLING_RECONCILIATION_MISMATCH` / `FORMAL_PREVIOUS_BILLING_RECONCILIATION_MISMATCH`).
  3. Added operator-facing `409` diagnostic messaging for `FORMAL_PRIOR_CERTIFICATE_UNRESOLVED` / `TEMPLATE_PRIOR_CERTIFICATE_UNRESOLVED` in `src/app/projects/[id]/uboq/templates/TemplateBuilder.tsx`.
  4. Verified in `WP3-BB-IR2-01` (`scripts/uboq/verify-wp3-batch-b-corrections.ts`) across mismatched prior `previousQuantityBilled` (`409` unresolved + `409` cumulative overbilling where skipping would pass), over-ceiling prior billing on same and cross-BOQ lines (`409`), insufficient-inspection and broken-history prior accomplishments (`409` unresolved + `409` cumulative overbilling where skipping would pass), audit-safe non-financial error payload checks, valid 3-bill / 4-accomplishment sequential chains (`200`), and cross-project isolation.

### `WP3-BB-IR2-02` — EVM Activity-Specific Certification & Verified Quantity Cap (`HIGH`)
- **Finding (`OSERP-WP3-BATCH-B-IR2-2026-11-14`)**: In `loadT7ScheduleEvmCanonicalData` (`src/lib/uboq/templates/canonical-source-adapters.ts`), `certLinkedUpdate` previously checked only whether `accomplishmentId` belonged to the project-wide `approvedAccomplishmentIds` set and then trusted `progressPercent` directly for `actualPctDec` and `EV`, without requiring activity-specific BOQ scope overlap or bounding `progressPercent` to inspected certified quantities allocated to that activity.
- **Closure**:
  1. Hardened `loadT7ScheduleEvmCanonicalData` in `src/lib/uboq/templates/canonical-source-adapters.ts` to build `activityMappedBoqIds` per activity (`uboqLinks` + `boqAllocations`) and `scheduleMappedBoqIds` across the schedule, and to validate project `APPROVED` accomplishments chronologically (`accomplishmentDate ASC, createdAt ASC, id ASC`) with inspection sufficiency, carried-forward continuity, ceiling checks, and `poisonedEvmBoqIds` propagation so broken historical chains cannot contribute EV.
  2. Restricted the unified `PV`/`EV`/`AC` cutoff (`statusCutoffDate <= now()`) strictly to pre-cutoff verified accomplishments certifying at least one BOQ item in `scheduleMappedBoqIds` and activity-matched cert updates (`activityCertUpdate`), preventing future-dated, out-of-schedule, or cross-activity approvals from shifting the schedule cutoff.
  3. Required `activityCertUpdate` to satisfy both `hasBoqScope` on the activity AND non-empty BOQ intersection between `activityMappedBoqIds` and the referenced `Accomplishment`'s verified certified BOQ items. Cross-activity updates (`mismatchedAccomplishmentUpdate`) and unlinked activities fail closed to `actual_progress_pct = 0.0000` and `earned_value = 0.00` while preserving `self_reported_progress_pct` (`UNVERIFIED_UNRELATED_ACCOMPLISHMENT_EXCLUDED_FROM_EV` / `UNVERIFIED_NO_ACTIVITY_BOQ_LINKAGE_EXCLUDED_FROM_EV`).
  4. Implemented two-stage shared phase-line / BOQ certified pool deduplication (`remainingPhaseLinePool` / `remainingBoqAllocPool`) and bounded EV-eligible progress strictly via `actualPctDec = min(claimedPctDec, verifiedShare.pct)` (`CERTIFIED_ACCOMPLISHMENT_CAPPED_BY_VERIFIED_BOQ_QTY` when capped, preserving `self_reported_progress_pct`).
  5. Verified in `WP3-BB-IR2-02` (`scripts/uboq/verify-wp3-batch-b-corrections.ts`) across cross-activity `100%` claim (`EV = 0.00`), unlinked activity `100%` claim (`EV = 0.00`), `10%` certified quantity vs `100%` `progressPercent` (`actual_progress_pct = 10.0000%`, `EV = 20,000.00`), valid activity-specific `40%` certification (`EV = 60,000.00`, `AC = 50,000.00`), and out-of-schedule/future cutoff isolation (`2026-09-15` preserved).

### `WP3-BB-IR3-01` — Single Authoritative Per-BOQ Certified Quantity Budget Across Link Methods & Snapshot Versions (`HIGH`)
- **Finding (`OSERP_WP3_BatchB_IR2_Independent_Review_2026-11-15`)**: In `loadT7ScheduleEvmCanonicalData` (`src/lib/uboq/templates/canonical-source-adapters.ts`), `T7_SCHEDULE_EVM` maintained separate certified-quantity pools (`remainingPhaseLinePool` for `UboqScheduleActivityLink` and `remainingBoqAllocPool` for `ScheduleBOQAllocation`) and initialized phase-line certification shares per `snapshotId::awardedBoqItemId`, allowing the same `AwardedBOQItem`'s certified quantity to be counted twice if mapped through both link representations or across multiple approved snapshot versions.
- **Closure**:
  1. Normalized every schedule activity's BOQ mappings into `NormalizedActivityBoqSlice`s (`UBOQ_PHASE_LINK` and `LEGACY_BOQ_ALLOCATION`), enforcing cross-project ownership (`403 TEMPLATE_SOURCE_OWNERSHIP_DENIED`), approved/superseded pre-wall-clock snapshot authority (`409 TEMPLATE_UNAPPROVED_EVM_SNAPSHOT_SOURCE`), deduplicating exact same-activity legacy shadow allocations while failing closed (`409 TEMPLATE_OVERLAPPING_EVM_BOQ_MAPPING`) on intra-activity conflicting link methods or multi-snapshot overlaps.
  2. Added schedule-wide ceiling and multi-snapshot phase-slot reconciliation per canonical `AwardedBOQItem` (`boqId`) before EV allocation: selects the single highest-version referenced snapshot (`authSnapId`) to define canonical phase slots (`phaseCode::sectionCode::itemCode`) **once** per BOQ item, failing closed (`409 TEMPLATE_OVERLAPPING_EVM_BOQ_MAPPING`) whenever combined mapped scope across activities, link types, or snapshot versions exceeds canonical phase-slot or BOQ ceilings (`totalScheduleMappedBoqScope > boqCeilingDec`).
  3. Replaced the dual pools with **one authoritative per-BOQ certified quantity budget** (`remainingBoqCertBudget` keyed by `AwardedBOQItem.id`) and **one canonical phase-slot sub-cap map** (`remainingCanonicalSlotCap`, derived once per BOQ item and never replenished by additional snapshot versions). All schedule activities and both linkage methods consume jointly from `remainingBoqCertBudget`, backed by a post-allocation invariant check (`totalCreditedForBoq <= maxBoqCertBudget`, `409 TEMPLATE_EVM_CERTIFIED_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`).
  4. Verified in `WP3-BB-IR3-01` (`scripts/uboq/verify-wp3-batch-b-corrections.ts`) across mixed `UboqScheduleActivityLink` + `ScheduleBOQAllocation` (`10` certified units -> `5.0000 + 5.0000 = 10.0000` units credited on `20`-unit BOQ; `409` on `10 + 10 > 10`-unit BOQ ceiling and intra-activity conflicts), multi-snapshot `v1` + `v2` (`5.0000 + 5.0000 = 10.0000` units with zero pool replenishment; `409` on `10 + 10 > 10`-unit ceiling), legitimate `60 + 40` split & distinct BOQ allocations (`110,000.00` EV), cross-project `403`, and `SITE_ENGINEER` financial redaction.
