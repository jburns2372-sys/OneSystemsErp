# OneSystemsERP — Monthly Closing Checklist

**Document Code:** CKL-003  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Classification:** Internal — Finance, Accounting, Project Management, Admin  
**Effective Date:** June 2026  

---

## Instructions

This checklist ensures a **complete and accurate month-end closing** across all ERP modules. Follow the sections in order — dependencies between steps require sequential completion.

**Target Completion:** Within **5 business days** after the last day of each month.
